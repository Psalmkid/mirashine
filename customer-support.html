Common questions

Which surfaces can I clean?

Use Mira Shine Wash for suitable everyday household and workplace cleaning tasks. For delicate or unfamiliar surfaces, test a small hidden area first.

Yes. Contact us directly for bulk quantities, availability and delivery arrangements.

How do I order?

Add products to your cart, then use the WhatsApp order option. Our team will confirm the order and delivery details.

What if I need help choosing a size?

Send us a message with what you clean and how often you use it. We can point you toward the most practical option.
